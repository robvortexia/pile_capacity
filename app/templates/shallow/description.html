Shallow foundations: load-settlement from CPT (demo)

Estimate the bearing-pressure versus settlement response of a pad or strip footing directly from CPT data, using the UWA/Lehane method. The tool reads your CPT, classifies the founding material from the average soil-behaviour-type index Ic in the zone of influence, then applies the sand or clay formulation:

  • Sand (Ic < 2.59): relative density and friction angle from qc, an excavation-unloading correction to qc, then a q–s curve scaled by qc,avg with a creep/time factor and an overconsolidation settlement offset.
  • Clay (Ic ≥ 2.59): net cone resistance and OCR give immediate and long-term q–s curves, capped at the ultimate bearing pressure.

This module is an internal demo and is not yet public.
