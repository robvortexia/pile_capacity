Lateral response of a monopile in sand

The program predicts the load-displacement and moment-rotation response of a monopile (with L/D < 5) subjected to an eccentric lateral load. The methodology follows that outlined in:

Wang, H., Lehane B.M., Bransby, M.F., Wang, L.Z., Hong, Y. and Askarinejad, A. (2023). Lateral behavior of monopiles in sand under monotonic loading. Ocean Engineering, 277, 114334.

Read the publication (PDF)

The ultimate lateral geotechnical capacity is also computed using the formulation in the following publication:

Wang, H., Lehane B.M., Bransby, M.F., Wang, L.Z. and Hong, Y. (2020). A simple approach for predicting the ultimate lateral capacity of a rigid pile in sand. Geotechnique Letters, 10, 429-435.

Read the publication (PDF)

Scope and limits

  • Sand or sandy soils — the average Ic over the embedded length must be below 2.2. The program rejects clay or silty profiles automatically.
  • Short piles with L/D less than 5. For higher aspect ratios, the program issues a warning as the assumption of a short pile may be invalid.
  • No load or material factors are applied. Bending stresses in the monopile section are not checked.
