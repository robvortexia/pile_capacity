Lateral response of a rigid monopile in sand

This program predicts the lateral displacement, rotation and moment-rotation response of a rigid monopile in sand subjected to a lateral load applied at or above ground level. The method uses CPT data to derive the small-strain shear modulus profile and idealises it as one of three best-fit forms (linear, power of 0.5, or constant). A hyperbolic stiffness reduction with rotation captures the non-linear response, with closed-form pile-flexibility corrections from Mindlin / Euler-Bernoulli added on top.

The ultimate geotechnical lateral capacity at very large rotation follows the Wang et al. (2020) p-y integration along the embedded length, split at the rotation point at 0.75L.

Scope and limits:

  • Sand or sandy soils — the average Ic over the embedded length must be below 2.2. The program rejects clay or silty profiles automatically.
  • Rigid monopiles — L/D below 5. For higher aspect ratios the program issues a warning and the rigid-pile assumption may not hold.
  • No load or material factors are applied. Bending stresses in the monopile section are not checked.
