Deflection of an embedded cantilever wall in sand

The program predicts the maximum lateral deflection of an embedded cantilever sheet-pile wall in unsaturated sand and the corresponding settlement profile behind it. The methodology follows the design charts presented in:

Lehane, B.M., Bagbag, A., Durham, C. and Pine, T. (2019). Design charts for lateral deflection of embedded cantilever walls in unsaturated Perth sands. Proceedings of the 13th Australia-New Zealand Conference on Geomechanics, Perth.

Read the publication (PDF)

The charts are entered with the average peak friction angle, the depth-gradient of qc, the H/L geometry ratio and the normalised wall flexural rigidity EI/(γw·H4), all derived from the CPT over a depth band of 1.5·H (clamped by the H/L bounds of the calibration). The settlement profile decays exponentially behind the wall with smax = 0.6·dmax (H/L > 0.5) or 0.5·dmax, and s ≈ 0.61·smax at a distance H/2 behind the wall.

Scope and limits

  • Sand profiles only — the average Ic over the averaging band must be below 2.2.
  • H/L in [0.2, 0.671]; EI/(γw·H4) in [5, 4500].
  • Water table at least 2·H below ground.
  • qc must increase with depth (dqc/dz > 0).
  • CPT data must reach the base of the wall.
  • No load or material factors. Bending stresses in the wall section are not checked.
